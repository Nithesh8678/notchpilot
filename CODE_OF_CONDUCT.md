# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository security reporting when enabled; do not post another person's private information in an issue. Reports will be handled with discretion and without retaliation.
